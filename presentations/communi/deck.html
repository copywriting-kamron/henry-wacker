03 · Community RelationshipscommUni

Five ways we build loyalty

For commUni it is important to build a strong relationship with our users and keep them interested in the app.

1

Community exchange & networking

Students connect with others who share similar interests – e.g. study groups or sports clubs.

2

Personalised recommendations

Every student has different needs, so we show relevant content: shared flats, second-hand furniture, tutoring offers.

3

Digital service & feedback

Users get help directly in the app and give feedback that helps us improve commUni.

4

Trust & safety

Moderation keeps the platform safe – especially when students look for flats or trade items with strangers.

5

User loyalty

Relevant content, regular updates and helpful reminders keep students coming back.
